# Teams

Teams are the primary unit of collaboration in the Codetopia Portal. Members can form teams to work together on projects, track contributions, and provide peer reviews.

## Creating a Team

Any member with the `teams.create` permission can create a new team. When you create a team:
1. You are automatically assigned as the **Team Lead**.
2. You define a **Member Tag** (e.g., "Dev", "Designer") which is used to label team members.
3. The system dynamically generates scoped roles and permissions for your team.

## Inviting Members

Team Leads can invite other community members to join their team:
1. Go to your Team Dashboard.
2. Click **Invite**.
3. Enter the username of the member you want to invite.
4. The member will receive an invite which they can accept or decline.

## Contribution Reviews

Reviews are the "Heartbeat" of a team. They allow members to self-report their work and get feedback from their peers and leads.

### Opening a Review
1. Go to your Team's **Reviews** page.
2. Click **Open Review**.
3. Provide a title, optional category, and a detailed description of your contribution.

### Reviewing & Approving
Team Leads and invited peers can comment on reviews. 
*   **Approve:** Once the work is satisfactory, the Team Lead can approve the review. 
*   **Contribution Graph:** Approved reviews are automatically tracked and displayed on your public profile's **Contribution Graph**.

## Scoped Permissions

Team access is governed by scoped permissions (e.g., `teams.view:123`). This ensures that being a lead in one team does not grant you management rights in another.
